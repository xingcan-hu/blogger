HBO Max plans can look deceptively simple until you need to decide whether ads, downloads, 4K, or simultaneous streams actually matter. This beginner's guide explains what each plan includes, how profiles work, what parents can restrict, and what to check before subscribing for shows such as *Friends* and *The Big Bang Theory*.

The prices and product details below were checked on August 14, 2026, using the U.S. HBO Max help center. Plans, catalogs, subtitles, and payment methods vary by country and can change.

## What HBO Max includes

HBO Max is Warner Bros. Discovery's general entertainment streaming service. It combines HBO series and films with Max Originals and selected content from Warner Bros., DC, Cartoon Network, Adult Swim, Discovery, and other brands. That makes it especially attractive to viewers who value established television series and Warner-owned franchises rather than simply the largest possible catalog.

The service works on supported phones, tablets, web browsers, televisions, streaming players, set-top boxes, and game consoles. Availability is regional. HBO Max's official [availability page](https://help.hbomax.com/us-en/Answer/Detail/000002518) explains that the catalog and ratings can change when you travel, and streaming stops when you enter a country where the service is unavailable.

Live and linear programming may still contain advertising, so an ad-free base plan should not be interpreted as a promise that every type of content is free of promotions.

### Which plan is the best value?

- Choose **Basic with Ads** when price matters most and interruptions are acceptable.
- Choose **Standard** for ad-free on-demand viewing and downloads on a phone or tablet. It is the most balanced choice for one or two viewers.
- Choose **Premium** when three or four people regularly stream at the same time, or when you have a 4K HDR television and compatible audio equipment.

Premium does not make every title 4K. If your main goal is to watch older sitcoms, the meaningful upgrade may be extra streams rather than picture quality. Standard is usually sufficient for a couple who watches together.

## Profiles are not the same as simultaneous streams

One HBO Max account can have up to five profiles. Each profile keeps its own avatar, My List, Continue Watching row, recommendations, display language, rating settings, and optional PIN. The official [profile guide](https://help.hbomax.com/Answer/Detail/000002539) covers how to create, edit, switch, and delete them.

Five profiles do not allow five people to stream at once. Basic with Ads and Standard are limited to two simultaneous streams; Premium allows four. When the account is already at its limit, another viewer may receive a too-many-devices message even if they are using a different profile.

To get started on a new device, install the official HBO Max app or visit HBOMax.com, sign in with the account email and password, and select your profile. A Profile PIN helps prevent another household member from changing your watch history or recommendations, but it does not create a separate subscription or reserve a streaming slot.

## Kids profiles and parental controls

HBO Max supports Adult and Kids profiles. According to the official [Kids profile documentation](https://help.hbomax.com/us-en/answer/detail/000002540), parents can choose rating groups ranging from Little Kids through Teens. Search results and the Kids home screen are then restricted to titles within the selected ratings. A child also gets a separate My List and Continue Watching row without access to password or subscription management.

Two controls are worth configuring together:

- **Kid-Proof Exit** requires the account's four-digit Parent Code before leaving a Kids profile.
- **Adult Profile PINs** help stop a child from switching directly into an unrestricted adult profile.

The Parent Code applies across the account, while a Profile PIN protects one profile. Device-level parental controls can impose stricter limits than HBO Max's own settings.

## Is HBO Max the right place for Friends and The Big Bang Theory?

*Friends* and *The Big Bang Theory* are major reasons many viewers first consider HBO Max. Both are closely associated with Warner's television library, but catalog rights remain regional and time-limited. A title appearing in the United States does not guarantee that it is included in another country's HBO Max catalog.

Before paying for a long subscription, use the HBO Max site or app for the country where you will actually watch and check:

1. whether the title appears in search;
2. whether all seasons are available;
3. which subtitle and audio languages appear on both early and late episodes;
4. whether the selected plan's ad and stream limits suit your household;
5. whether another local rights holder currently carries the show.

This is more reliable than buying from an old list of “included” shows. Streaming rights can move even when the service itself remains available.

## Classic series, originals, and movies to start with

New subscribers can use HBO's best-known series as a map through the catalog:

- **Foundational HBO drama:** *The Sopranos*, *The Wire*, *Six Feet Under*, *Band of Brothers*, and *Sex and the City*.
- **Modern prestige series:** *Game of Thrones*, *House of the Dragon*, *Succession*, *The Last of Us*, *The White Lotus*, and *True Detective*.
- **Comedy:** *Friends*, *The Big Bang Theory*, and *Curb Your Enthusiasm*.
- **Recent HBO and Max originals:** titles such as *The Pitt*, with seasons and release dates depending on location.
- **Film franchises:** selected films from Harry Potter, the DC Universe, The Lord of the Rings, and Warner Bros.' library.

HBO Max's own [service overview](https://help.hbomax.com/us-en/Answer/Detail/000002543) describes a mixture of HBO, Max Originals, Warner Bros., DC, Cartoon Network, and Discovery content. It is sensible to think of the service as a major home for those brands, but not as a permanent archive of every Warner film. Theatrical films are not automatically guaranteed to arrive on the service on release day.

## Regional access, payment, and mainland China

HBO Max is available in the United States, Latin America, the Caribbean, and many countries and regions across Europe and Asia Pacific. Mainland China is not listed as a supported territory as of the check date. Hong Kong, Macau, and Taiwan do appear in the Asia Pacific list, but they are separate service regions.

For direct U.S. web subscriptions, HBO Max lists Visa, Mastercard, Discover, American Express, JCB, PayPal, and Apple Pay. It also states that credit or debit cards must be U.S.-issued and support recurring payments. App stores, mobile operators, and television providers apply their own regional payment rules.

Users in mainland China therefore face more than a payment problem: the registration region, payment method, actual viewing location, and regional catalog must all be supported. HBO Max says VPNs, proxies, and anonymization services should be disabled when resolving location errors; this guide does not provide instructions for bypassing regional controls.

## What a FamilyPro shared profile changes

[FamilyPro's HBO Max listing](https://familypro.io/en/products/max) currently sells one profile within a shared account rather than ownership of the main account. Its stated process is to purchase a seat, receive the shared credentials through the order dashboard or email, sign in through the official HBO Max app or website, and select the assigned profile.

That can reduce the payment setup required, but it introduces tradeoffs that do not exist with a direct personal subscription:

- the customer does not control the account email, password, devices, or subscription;
- changing the shared password can void third-party support;
- Premium's four-stream allowance is shared by the entire account, not granted separately to every purchased profile;
- profile retention, renewal, and support depend on the seller;
- FamilyPro's statements about official access, availability, and refunds are its own marketing claims and were not independently stress-tested for this article.

Anyone considering a shared account should read the current purchase policy, keep the order record, avoid reusing personal passwords, and confirm that HBO Max officially supports the place where the service will be used.

## The simple buying decision

Basic with Ads is the budget choice. Standard is the practical default for one or two people who want downloads and ad-free on-demand television. Premium earns its higher price when a household genuinely needs more than two simultaneous streams or regularly watches supported 4K HDR titles.

Before subscribing for one specific show, verify the current local catalog and subtitle options. Before choosing a shared profile, decide whether the lower setup barrier is worth giving up control over the main account and its streaming capacity.
